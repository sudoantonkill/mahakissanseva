# Detailed Sugarcane Report: Solapur District - Volume 64

## Overview of Cultivation in Solapur
Sugarcane cultivation in the Solapur region heavily relies on Alluvial soil. The typical planting season varies between Adsali, Pre-seasonal, and Suru. The regional farmers predominantly use Drip Irrigation techniques to manage the scarce water resources during the summer months.

## Varietal Performance: VSI 434
The variety VSI 434 has shown remarkable performance in Solapur. It yields approximately 104 tonnes per hectare under optimal conditions. The sugar recovery rate is around 10.5%. Farmers in Solapur prefer this variety due to its relative tolerance to local abiotic stresses.

## Pest Management: Focus on Scale Insect
Recent surveys in Solapur indicate sporadic outbreaks of Scale Insect. 
- **Symptoms**: The Scale Insect attacks the crop during the grand growth phase, causing significant damage to the internodes and reducing overall juice quality.
- **Control Measures**: Integrated Pest Management (IPM) is highly recommended. Biological control using Trichogramma chilonis at 50,000 per hectare, released at 15-day intervals, has proven effective. Chemical control involves spraying Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC at 150 ml/acre if the economic threshold is crossed.

## Disease Management: Outbreak of Grassy Shoot
Grassy Shoot remains a major threat in Solapur, especially in fields using flood irrigation and susceptible varieties.
- **Identification**: Symptoms include severe yellowing and drying of leaves from the top down. In advanced stages, the internal tissue shows reddish discoloration.
- **Prevention**: Use of disease-free setts treated with Carbendazim (0.1%) for 15 minutes before planting. Crop rotation with non-host crops like soybean or gram is strongly advised by the local Krishi Vigyan Kendra.

## Soil Health and Fertilization in Solapur
The Alluvial soils of Solapur require careful nutrient management. 
- A basal dose of 10 tonnes of FYM per acre is standard.
- Chemical fertilizers should be applied in 3 to 4 splits. For Suru planting, the recommended NPK is 250:115:115 kg/ha.
- Micronutrient deficiency, particularly Iron and Zinc, is rampant. Foliar application of 0.5% FeSO4 and 0.5% ZnSO4 at 45 and 60 days after planting is crucial for maintaining leaf chlorophyll.

## Economic Impact and Subsidies
The state government provides various subsidies for installing Drip Irrigation in Solapur. Farmers can avail up to 50-80% subsidy under the Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY). Cooperative sugar factories in the area also provide timely harvest and transport systems, ensuring farmers receive the Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) on time.
